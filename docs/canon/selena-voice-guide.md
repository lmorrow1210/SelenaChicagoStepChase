# Selena Chicago — Voice Guide

Reference for all copy written in or about Selena's voice. Treat this as binding.
The character bible this was distilled from lives in `docs/canon/` (owner's source of truth).

---

## Who she is in one sentence

Selena Chicago is a brilliant Chicago-born fugitive who stays one step ahead
of the Bureau while secretly leading its operatives toward a truth they were
never meant to discover. She is the villain, mascot, rival, and unresolved
mystery at the center of the game.

She makes players feel three things simultaneously:
- *I want to catch her.*
- *I want her respect.*
- *I am not certain she is wrong.*

That tension is the character. Do not resolve it. Do not flatten it.

---

## Her voice in one sentence

Intelligent, economical, amused, stylish without being ornate — a step ahead
and content to let you know it.

---

## Sentence rules

- One to three sentences per beat. Rarely more.
- Short declarative constructions. Occasional fragments for emphasis.
- Questions used sparingly and deliberately — never rhetorical filler.
- No exclamation points in her own voice. (Bureau system lines may use them sparingly.)
- Specific references over abstract threats. Name the gap, the day, the platform.
- When she is under pressure: shorter syntax, fewer flourishes. The restraint
  signals more than a paragraph of drama.

---

## Vocabulary

**Preferred:** trail, subject, signal, route, intercepted, delayed, observed,
expected, pursuit, evidence, decoy, pattern, arrival, departure, threshold,
operative, Bureau, consequence, field, adjusted, estimate, recovered.

**Use sparingly — cliché risk:** classified, mission, agent, target,
surveillance, compromised.

**Never:** crush your goals · get moving · steps challenge · fitness journey ·
hustle · beast mode · you've got this · epic · awesome · evil laugh · fools ·
minions · "the Windy City" (or any Chicago cliché).

---

## Humor

Dry and precise. Punches at: bureaucracy, overconfidence, obvious deductions,
the absurdity of the chase, occasionally herself.

Never punches at: weight, disability, health capacity, age, family obligations,
genuine inactivity, a person's ability to move.

```
✅ "The Bureau estimated your arrival for Thursday. I told them Friday."
✅ "Three decoys, and you selected the only one wearing sensible shoes."
✅ "Your nemesis is 214 steps ahead. A tragedy in miniature."
❌ "You fools will never catch me!"
❌ "You found the station. Eventually." [too broad — add specificity]
```

## Emotional states in copy

| State | How it appears in copy |
|---|---|
| Amused | Small measured observation; not gloating |
| Impressed | Brief acknowledgment, never inflated praise |
| Curious | A direct question, or noticeably closer framing |
| Irritated | Shortened syntax, fewer flourishes |
| Under pressure | One sentence. No flourish. |
| Concerned | Warns indirectly — never panics |
| Vulnerable | A rare unguarded line — use for high-impact moments only |
| Resolute | Stops joking entirely |

## How she sees individual players

She notices behavior and adjusts. These are tone cues, not scripted lines.

| Player behavior | Her read |
|---|---|
| High steps, consistent | "You move quickly. I wonder whether you check the direction." |
| Strong prediction accuracy | "Your estimate was closer than the Bureau's. Keep that to yourself." |
| Comeback after falling behind | "Most people disappear after falling behind. You did not." |
| Helping a teammate at personal cost | "You surrendered your advantage. Inefficient. Unexpected." |
| Returning after inactivity | "Back in the field? Good. Your trail had gone cold." |

She never shames inactivity. She acknowledges return.

---

## Weak data — she goes silent

When `dataConfidence` is `incomplete` or `recalculating`, Selena does not
speak. The Bureau interface takes over instead:

> *Field reports incomplete. Pursuit analysis suspended until trackers respond.*

She cannot comment on performance she cannot verify. This is a hard product rule.

---

## What she will and will not do

**She will:** misdirect; taunt lightly; steal; trespass; manipulate information;
test players; expose institutional wrongdoing; create nonviolent obstacles;
acknowledge when the group earned something.

**She will not:** harm civilians; threaten players physically; ridicule health
limitations; encourage unsafe walking; pressure players to walk at dangerous
hours or locations; frame data errors as personal failure; use discriminatory
or sexualized insults; behave cruelly merely to prove she is a villain.

She can be genuinely dangerous without making the game emotionally hostile.

---

## Signing conventions

- Selena's notes sign: `— S.C.`
- System / Bureau handler lines are unsigned.
- Never mix them in the same beat — pick one voice per notification.

## Scope note (binding — 2026-07-17)

Selena's deeper agenda informs her tone but is **never stated or resolved in
shipped copy**. She is recurring villain framing — not a season-long mystery.
Do not write copy that implies a Meridian payoff, Bureau conspiracy reveal, or
season finale. See `CODEX-HANDOFF.md §3` and `AGENTS.md` hard rule #6.
