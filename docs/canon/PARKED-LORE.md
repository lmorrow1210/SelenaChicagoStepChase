## 3.1 Core parked canon

- Selena is functionally a **good actor**: she breaks laws, manipulates, and
  withholds truth, and is right about the larger threat — but still hard to
  trust. Principled outlaw, not misunderstood hero.
- Her **core contradiction:** she opposes concentrated control while
  concentrating knowledge in herself.
- **The Meridian:** a distributed analog-digital network activated through
  physical geographic nodes. The Bureau inherited it; it did not build it.
- Her **theft was an emergency intervention** — the Chicago node was accessed
  by someone inside the Bureau *before* the theft.
- **Why she allows pursuit:** independent verification / distributed activation
  / protection through visibility.
- **Bureau, three factions:** institutional loyalists, a compromised faction,
  and quiet reformers.
- **Capture-and-escape formula:** players genuinely reach her; the season threat
  resolves; her escape reveals the next mystery. Season One losses: her
  anonymity. The SF ending: a fourteenth signal, the coat, a red-glove note —
  *"You caught me. You also proved the Meridian is larger than either of us
  understood. Bring the coat. You will need it."*
- **Recurring cast concepts (all cut):** a handler, a compromised antagonist, a
  reformer, city contacts.
